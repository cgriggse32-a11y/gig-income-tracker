Free tool for drivers

Know your real dollars per hour.

Gig Income Tracker is a free, private app for DoorDash, Walmart Spark, Uber Eats, Amazon Flex and Roadie drivers. Log every shift — see exactly what each app actually pays you.

  • Log shifts in seconds — hours, base pay, tips, miles, gas & expenses.
  • True $/hour and $/mile — after expenses, per platform, so you know which app wins.
  • 100% private — no account, no server, your data never leaves your phone. Export to CSV at tax time.
